Focus

Accessibility

At Bandai Namco Europe I was on the accessibility task force that worked on the publisher's accessibility strategy. I studied what the industry does best, from The Last of Us Part II to Tekken 8, mapped the options players need, and wrote the team's must-have accessibility document.

On Little Nightmares I turned an accessibility audit into a prioritised backlog that the team shipped. Today I bring the same approach to the studios I advise and to my students.
