Focus

Accessibility

At Bandai Namco Europe I sat on the accessibility task force that worked on the publisher's accessibility strategy. I benchmarked accessibility in leading games, mapped player needs across vision, hearing, motor, cognition and VR comfort, and wrote the team's list of must-have features.

I bring the same approach to the studios I advise and to my students. Accessibility belongs in the first prototype, not in a patch before launch.

  1. BenchmarkCase studies from The Last of Us Part II and Tekken 8 to every Little Nightmares game, VR included.
  2. Must-havesThe team's baseline of accessibility features, organised by player need.
  3. ShippedOn Little Nightmares, an accessibility audit became a prioritised backlog that the team delivered.

    Global Associate Producer · Bandai Namco Entertainment Europe

    Lyon, France · Little Nightmares franchise

    Part of the core team behind the franchise's worldwide strategy.     I tracked milestones and deliveries, kept QA, analytics, localisation, marketing and communications aligned, and reported progress to stakeholders and the IP holder. I rebuilt project tracking in Jira, Confluence and SharePoint, automated a weekly report that used to take a full day, and kept scope consistent across the main game, VR and mobile.
